About This Website

I created this website as a central place to showcase my journey as a Cybersecurity and Criminology student at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS). The website highlights my education, certifications, projects, home lab, practical cybersecurity experience, and professional aspirations. It serves as both a portfolio for recruiters and a personal platform to document my growth as I continue developing technical and analytical skills.

The website was built using HTML and CSS and is hosted through GitHub Pages. Building it gave me the opportunity to strengthen my web development skills while creating a professional online presence that reflects my interests and achievements. It is designed to evolve alongside my studies, certifications, and future projects.

My goal is to combine knowledge from both Cybersecurity and Criminology to better understand cybercrime, digital threats, public safety, investigation, and risk. I am particularly interested in areas such as Security Operations (SOC), Threat Intelligence, Incident Response, Cybercrime Analysis, Digital Forensics, and Cybersecurity Research. By studying both disciplines together, I hope to develop a broader understanding of not only how cyber threats occur, but also the human, social, and criminal factors behind them.

Looking ahead, I plan to expand this portfolio with additional cybersecurity and criminology projects, including home lab documentation, threat intelligence reports, incident response case studies, digital forensics exercises, TryHackMe and Hack The Box write-ups, cybercrime analysis articles, and research into crime prevention and public safety. My aim is to continuously learn, build practical skills, and contribute to both the cybersecurity and criminology fields.
